# Bloom Fisching (browser game)

Open `index.html` in any browser (desktop or phone). No install needed.

**How to play**
1. Hold (mouse, touch, or Space) to charge your cast, release to throw.
2. Wait for the `!` bite, then tap fast.
3. Reeling: hold to lift the bar, let go to drop it. Keep the fish inside the bar until the progress meter fills.

Sell catches automatically for coins, buy better rods in the Shop (bigger bar, more luck, faster reeling), and fill your Journal with all 10 fish. Progress saves in your browser.
